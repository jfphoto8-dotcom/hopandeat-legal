🍺 Privacy Policy — HopAndEat

Last updated: November 7, 2026
Version: 2.1

This Privacy Policy explains how HopAndEat collects, uses, and protects your personal information, in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Spanish law.

1. Data Controller

Name: Jonatan Fernández Blanco
Contact: support@hopandeat.app
Country: Spain

2. What is HopAndEat

HopAndEat is a mobile application for iOS and Android that helps users find nearby restaurants and bars, view ratings and reviews, and save their favourite places. The app uses artificial intelligence to generate review summaries.

3. Data We Collect and Legal Basis

3.1 Location

We request access to your GPS location to show nearby restaurants. Legal basis: Performance of contract / legitimate interest. Your location is sent directly from your device to Google Maps Platform to run each search, and is never stored on our servers. You can revoke access at any time in your device settings.

3.2 Account Data

An account is required to use the app. We collect your name, email address, optional profile photo, and authentication provider (Apple, Google or email). Legal basis: Performance of contract.

3.3 Favourites and Preferences

From version 1.2.1, for each favourite we store only the Google place ID, the city and the date you saved it; the name, rating and photo are fetched live from Google Maps Platform when you view them. Favourites saved with earlier versions may also hold that information until we remove it. Your language and theme preferences stay on your device. Legal basis: Performance of contract / consent.

3.4 Subscription Data

Payment data for Premium subscriptions is handled exclusively by Apple App Store or Google Play. We only receive subscription status confirmation via RevenueCat, and our server checks it before generating an AI summary. Legal basis: Performance of contract.

3.5 Artificial Intelligence (Review Summaries)

HopAndEat uses AI (Anthropic's Claude API) to generate automatic summaries of publicly available restaurant reviews retrieved from Google Maps. This feature:

  • Is available exclusively to Premium users
  • Processes public Google Maps reviews, not personal user data
  • Does not store the underlying reviews or the generated summaries on our servers beyond the time strictly needed to generate and deliver the response
  • Relies on an automated language model and may produce summaries that are incomplete, outdated, or factually inaccurate
  • Is provided for general informational and convenience purposes only, and does not constitute advice of any kind (culinary, dietary, allergen-related, medical, or otherwise)
Important: You are solely responsible for any decision you make in reliance on an AI-generated summary, including decisions related to allergens, dietary restrictions, opening hours, pricing, or the suitability of an establishment. HopAndEat is not liable for any loss, damage, or harm arising from reliance on AI-generated content. Always verify important information directly with the establishment before visiting.

Legal basis: Legitimate interest / implicit consent upon subscribing to Premium.

3.6 Crash and Diagnostic Data

We use Sentry to detect and fix errors. Crash reports and performance data include technical information such as your device model, operating system and app version. Legal basis: Legitimate interest in keeping the app working and secure.

3.7 Preventing Abuse

When you sign up with email, we use your IP address to limit how many accounts can be created from one network, and we check the email domain against a list of disposable addresses. We also keep a daily count of AI summaries per user to enforce the limit of 10 a day. Legal basis: Legitimate interest in protecting the service.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

HopAndEat is a native mobile application and does not use cookies, web trackers, pixels, or third-party advertising identifiers of any kind. We use local storage on your own device (AsyncStorage) solely to remember your session, language, theme, profile picture and setup status. From version 1.2.1, restaurant data and photos from Google Maps are held in memory only and are not saved to your device. This local data stays on your device, is never transmitted to our servers as a tracking mechanism, and is never shared with third parties for advertising or profiling purposes. If a future web version of HopAndEat introduces cookies, this section will be updated and, where legally required, accompanied by a cookie consent mechanism.

6. Third-Party Data Accuracy and Limitation of Liability

Restaurant names, addresses, opening hours, photos, ratings, and reviews displayed in HopAndEat are sourced from Google Maps Platform and reflect data supplied by Google and by third parties (restaurant owners, other platform users). HopAndEat:

  • Does not independently verify, edit, or guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or currency of this third-party data
  • Has no control over, and assumes no responsibility for, errors, omissions, or changes in this data (for example, a restaurant that has closed, changed its hours, or changed its menu)
  • Is not responsible for the content of third-party reviews, which express the opinions of their individual authors and not of HopAndEat

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, HopAndEat disclaims all liability for any loss or damage resulting from your reliance on restaurant information, ratings, reviews, or AI-generated summaries derived from this third-party data. This section does not affect any statutory rights that cannot be excluded under Spanish or EU consumer protection law.

7. Children's Privacy / Minimum Age

HopAndEat is not directed at children under 13 years old, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data to us, contact us at support@hopandeat.app and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union / European Economic Area, you have the following rights under the GDPR:

  • Access — obtain confirmation of, and access to, the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten") — request deletion of your data
  • Restriction of processing
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdrawal of consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal
  • Not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that produces legal or similarly significant effects — note that AI-generated review summaries are purely informational and do not involve automated decision-making about you as an individual

You can exercise the right to erasure directly and immediately using Profile → Delete account in the app. To exercise any other right, contact us at support@hopandeat.app. We will respond within one month (extendable by a further two months for complex requests, as permitted by GDPR Article 12).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD): www.aepd.es, or with the data protection authority of your EU member state of residence.

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures including HTTPS/TLS encryption and Firebase access rules. Esta política de privacidad explica cómo HopAndEat recopila, usa y protege tu información personal, de conformidad con el Reglamento General de Protección de Datos (GDPR) y la legislación española aplicable.

1. Responsable del tratamiento

Nombre: Jonatan Fernández Blanco
Email: support@hopandeat.app
País: España

2. Qué es HopAndEat

HopAndEat es una aplicación móvil disponible en iOS y Android que permite encontrar restaurantes y bares cercanos, ver valoraciones y reseñas, y guardar favoritos. La app utiliza inteligencia artificial para generar resúmenes de reseñas.

3. Datos que recopilamos y base legal

3.1 Ubicación

Solicitamos acceso a tu GPS para mostrarte restaurantes cercanos. Base legal: Ejecución del contrato / interés legítimo. Tu ubicación se envía directamente desde tu dispositivo a Google Maps Platform para cada búsqueda y nunca se almacena en nuestros servidores. Puedes revocar el acceso en los ajustes de tu dispositivo.

3.2 Datos de cuenta

Necesitas una cuenta para usar la app. Recopilamos tu nombre, email, foto de perfil (opcional) y proveedor de autenticación (Apple, Google o email). Base legal: Ejecución del contrato.

3.3 Favoritos y preferencias

Desde la versión 1.2.1, de cada favorito guardamos solo el identificador del sitio en Google, la ciudad y la fecha; el nombre, la valoración y la foto se consultan en directo en Google Maps Platform al verlos. Los favoritos guardados con versiones anteriores pueden conservar esa información hasta que la eliminemos. Tus preferencias de idioma y tema se quedan en tu dispositivo. Base legal: Ejecución del contrato / consentimiento.

3.4 Datos de suscripción

Los datos de pago Premium son gestionados exclusivamente por Apple App Store o Google Play. Solo recibimos confirmación del estado de tu suscripción a través de RevenueCat, y nuestro servidor la comprueba antes de generar un resumen IA. Base legal: Ejecución del contrato.

3.5 Inteligencia Artificial (resúmenes de reseñas)

HopAndEat utiliza IA (API de Claude de Anthropic) para generar resúmenes automáticos de reseñas públicas obtenidas de Google Maps. Esta función:

  • Está disponible exclusivamente para usuarios Premium
  • Procesa reseñas públicas de Google Maps, no datos personales del usuario
  • No almacena las reseñas procesadas ni los resúmenes generados en nuestros servidores más allá del tiempo estrictamente necesario para generar y entregar la respuesta
  • Se basa en un modelo de lenguaje automatizado y puede generar resúmenes incompletos, desactualizados o inexactos
  • Se ofrece únicamente con fines informativos y de conveniencia, y no constituye asesoramiento de ningún tipo (culinario, dietético, sobre alérgenos, médico u otro)
Importante: Eres el único responsable de cualquier decisión que tomes basándote en un resumen generado por IA, incluidas decisiones relacionadas con alérgenos, restricciones dietéticas, horarios, precios o la idoneidad de un establecimiento. HopAndEat no se hace responsable de ninguna pérdida, daño o perjuicio derivado de confiar en contenido generado por IA. Verifica siempre la información importante directamente con el establecimiento antes de acudir.

Base legal: Interés legítimo / consentimiento implícito al contratar Premium.

3.6 Datos de fallos y diagnóstico

Usamos Sentry para detectar y corregir errores. Los informes de fallos y los datos de rendimiento incluyen información técnica como el modelo del dispositivo, el sistema operativo y la versión de la app. Base legal: Interés legítimo en mantener la app funcionando y segura.

3.7 Prevención de abusos

Al registrarte con correo usamos tu dirección IP para limitar cuántas cuentas se crean desde una misma red, y comprobamos el dominio del correo frente a una lista de direcciones desechables. También guardamos un contador diario de resúmenes IA por usuario para aplicar el límite de 10 al día. Base legal: Interés legítimo en proteger el servicio.

4. Cookies y almacenamiento local

HopAndEat es una aplicación móvil nativa y no utiliza cookies, rastreadores web, píxeles ni identificadores publicitarios de terceros de ningún tipo. Usamos almacenamiento local en tu propio dispositivo (AsyncStorage) únicamente para recordar tu sesión, idioma, tema, foto de perfil y estado de configuración. Desde la versión 1.2.1, los datos y fotos de restaurantes de Google Maps se mantienen solo en memoria y no se guardan en tu dispositivo. Estos datos permanecen en tu dispositivo, nunca se transmiten a nuestros servidores como mecanismo de seguimiento y nunca se comparten con terceros con fines publicitarios o de perfilado. Si en el futuro se lanza una versión web de HopAndEat que utilice cookies, esta sección se actualizará y, cuando la ley lo exija, se acompañará de un mecanismo de consentimiento de cookies.

Si te encuentras en la Unión Europea / Espacio Económico Europeo, tienes los siguientes derechos conforme al GDPR:

  • Acceso — obtener confirmación y acceso a los datos personales que tratamos sobre ti
  • Rectificación — corregir datos inexactos o incompletos
  • Supresión ("derecho al olvido")
  • Limitación del tratamiento
  • Portabilidad — recibir tus datos en un formato estructurado y de uso común
