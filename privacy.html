🍺 Privacy Policy — Hop&Eat

Last updated: August 28, 2026
Version: 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how Hop&Eat collects, uses, and protects your personal information, in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Spanish law.

1. Data Controller

Name: Jonatan Fernández Blanco
Contact: support@hopandeat.app
Country: Spain

2. What is Hop&Eat

Hop&Eat is a mobile application for iOS and Android that helps users find nearby restaurants and bars, view ratings and reviews, and save their favourite places. The app uses artificial intelligence to generate review summaries.

3. Data We Collect and Legal Basis

3.1 Location

We request access to your GPS location to show nearby restaurants. Legal basis: Performance of contract / legitimate interest. Location is processed in real time and never stored on our servers. You can revoke access at any time in your device settings.

3.2 Account Data

If you register, we collect your name, email address, optional profile photo, and authentication provider (Apple, Google or email). Legal basis: Performance of contract.

3.3 Favourites and Preferences

We store your saved restaurants and preferences (language, theme). Legal basis: Performance of contract / consent.

3.4 Subscription Data

Payment data for Premium subscriptions is handled exclusively by Apple App Store or Google Play. We only receive subscription status confirmation via RevenueCat. Legal basis: Performance of contract.

3.5 Artificial Intelligence

Hop&Eat uses AI (Anthropic's Claude API) to generate automatic summaries of restaurant reviews. This feature:

  • Is available exclusively to Premium users
  • Processes public Google Places reviews, not personal user data
  • Does not store processed reviews or generated summaries on our servers
  • Generated summaries may contain inaccuracies

Legal basis: Legitimate interest / implicit consent upon subscribing to Premium.

4. Cookies

Hop&Eat is a mobile app and does not use tracking cookies. We use local device storage (AsyncStorage) solely to save your session, language, and theme preferences. This data is never shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

5. Third-Party Services and International Transfers

Some providers are located outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards (EU Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions).

6. Minimum Age

Hop&Eat is not directed at children under 13 years old. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data, contact us at support@hopandeat.app to have it deleted.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are an EU resident, you have the right to: access, rectification, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), data portability, objection, restriction, and to withdraw consent at any time.

To delete your account and all data: Profile → Delete account in the app.

To exercise any other right, contact us at support@hopandeat.app. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD): www.aepd.es.

🍺 Política de Privacidad — Hop&Eat

Esta política de privacidad explica cómo Hop&Eat recopila, usa y protege tu información personal, de conformidad con el Reglamento General de Protección de Datos (GDPR) y la legislación española aplicable.

1. Responsable del tratamiento

Nombre: Jonatan Fernández Blanco
Email: support@hopandeat.app
País: España

2. Qué es Hop&Eat

Hop&Eat es una aplicación móvil disponible en iOS y Android que permite encontrar restaurantes y bares cercanos, ver valoraciones y reseñas, y guardar favoritos. La app utiliza inteligencia artificial para generar resúmenes de reseñas.

3. Datos que recopilamos y base legal

3.1 Ubicación

Solicitamos acceso a tu GPS para mostrarte restaurantes cercanos. Base legal: Ejecución del contrato / interés legítimo. No almacenamos tu ubicación en nuestros servidores. Puedes revocar el acceso en los ajustes de tu dispositivo.

3.2 Datos de cuenta

Si te registras, recopilamos tu nombre, email, foto de perfil (opcional) y proveedor de autenticación (Apple, Google o email). Base legal: Ejecución del contrato.

3.3 Favoritos y preferencias

Guardamos los restaurantes favoritos y tus preferencias de idioma y tema. Base legal: Ejecución del contrato / consentimiento.

3.4 Datos de suscripción

Los datos de pago Premium son gestionados exclusivamente por Apple App Store o Google Play. Solo recibimos confirmación del estado de tu suscripción a través de RevenueCat. Base legal: Ejecución del contrato.

3.5 Inteligencia Artificial

Hop&Eat utiliza IA (API de Claude de Anthropic) para generar resúmenes automáticos de reseñas. Esta función:

  • Está disponible exclusivamente para usuarios Premium
  • Procesa reseñas públicas de Google Places, no datos personales del usuario
  • No almacena las reseñas procesadas ni los resúmenes generados en nuestros servidores
  • Los resúmenes pueden contener imprecisiones

Base legal: Interés legítimo / consentimiento implícito al contratar Premium.

4. Cookies

Hop&Eat es una app móvil y no utiliza cookies de seguimiento. Usamos almacenamiento local del dispositivo (AsyncStorage) únicamente para guardar tu sesión, idioma y tema. Estos datos nunca se comparten con terceros con fines publicitarios.
